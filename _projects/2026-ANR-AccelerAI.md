The objective of the project is to enable the adaptive co-construction of Ethics in and for a long-lived intelligent system. 
You can find more information about the project on the [Acceler-AI page itself](https://acceler-ai.projet.liris.cnrs.fr/). The project was funded by ANR (Agence Nationale de la Recherche).


Within this project, as a post-doc I am working on Work Package 3 (WP3) addressing Multi-Agent and Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning, to guide problem-solving under the co-existence of multiple individuals or groups with different values.
